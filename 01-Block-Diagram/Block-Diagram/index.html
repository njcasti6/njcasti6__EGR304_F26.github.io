Overview

The weight sensing board detects when a dose is lifted out of the cup. A 100 g load cell feeds an AD623 instrumentation amplifier and an RC low-pass filter into the ADC of a PIC18F57Q43 Curiosity Nano. The board reports status to the hub (Reminder & Alerts) over an 8-pin ribbon cable.
