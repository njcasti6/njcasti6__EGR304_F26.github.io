## Introduction

This datasheet documents my individual design work for Team 102's Project Aurora. It covers the weight sensing board from block diagram, component selection, bill of materials, schematic, and power budget. The datasheets can be used to understand what the board does, why each part was chosen, and how the board connects to the rest of the system.

### Project Summary

The weight sensing board will measure the weight of the prescription on a 100 g load cell and turns that measurement into a clean, usable signal. Thus signal will be to tell when the prescription has be dispensed and removed to the team hub.
